How it works

  1. Sign up. The scan becomes a code. That code is locked with a secret key. The picture is thrown away.
  2. Log in. A new scan tries to open the same lock. Small changes, like a tilt or a scratch, can still pass.
  3. Prove it. The system checks a short proof that the key was opened, without showing the key or the fingerprint.

What is kept: a lock, a checksum, and a public key. Not the photo, not the secret key.
